# ArticleCard

An editorial card for the Insights journal: a 4:3 image, a category chip, a serif title and a metadata line.

**Markup:** `<a class="kc-card">` wrapping `.kc-media` (an `<img>` or an `ImagePlaceholder`), a `.kc-chip` category, an `h3` and a `.kc-meta` line. The whole card is the link.

- The categories are Leadership, Personal Development, Communication, Language Learning, and Speaking & Ideas.
- On hover, the image eases to 1.03 scale over `duration-reveal` and the title turns `forest`.
- In grids, use one large lead card spanning seven columns beside two stacked cards. Avoid uniform rows of identical cards.
- Use only real articles or posts, and link to their source if they live elsewhere (LinkedIn, Instagram).
