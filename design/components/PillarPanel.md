# PillarPanel

A full-bleed band introducing one of the three professional pillars. Each pillar owns one dark ground, while sharing the same grid, numeral and type.

**Markup:** `<section class="kc-pillar kc-pillar--leadership|voice|language kc-on-ground">` with two children. The first holds a `.kc-num` numeral (01–03), an `.kc-eyebrow`, an `h3` and an optional `.kc-rule`. The second holds the copy and one CTA. For Speaking & Interpretation, use `.kc-split` so the two services stay distinct, each with its own CTA.

- Leadership & Coaching uses `forest-ground`, Speaking & Interpretation uses `charcoal-ground`, and Language Education uses `slate-ground`.
- On the homepage, separate pillars with a portrait or an ivory section. Never place them back to back unless they are framed as a single stacked set with `space-1` between them.
- **Motion:** the numeral and title rise on entry, and the copy follows one `duration-stagger` later. The image, if any, unmasks upward.
- Keep copy to two sentences. Facts that aren't confirmed go in as `[OWNER TO CONFIRM: …]`.
